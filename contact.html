Frequently Asked Questions

How quickly can you deploy guards to a new site?

For emergency deployments, we can have personnel on-site within 4-6 hours depending on the location. For standard contracts, we require 48 hours for proper site surveying and brief drafting.

Do you provide armed security personnel?

Yes. We have a highly trained division of armed protection specialists, primarily comprised of former military and law enforcement personnel, licensed to operate in specific jurisdictions.

How do you verify the background of your staff?

We conduct exhaustive 5-point background checks including criminal history, employment verification, drug screening, psychological evaluation, and local police verification before hiring.
